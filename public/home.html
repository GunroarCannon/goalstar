A* Pathfinding for Life Goals, Backed by Portable Memory

GoalStar maps the path between where you are right now and where you want to be. Give it your starting point and destination, and it drafts the realistic milestones in between. It estimates what each step costs in time, money, effort, and risk, then highlights the clearest route forward.

What it is

A step-by-step visual map. Every card is an actual milestone you can finish, and every line connects actions in an order that makes sense.

What it does

It runs pathfinding tuned to your daily stamina. When a heavy task risks burnout, it reroutes you through lighter, steady steps so you keep moving.

Use it for

Any goal with more than one route forward: getting a raise, studying overseas, launching a product, getting fit, or meeting someone.
