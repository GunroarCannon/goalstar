          Interactive Interface Guide

          What Each GoalStar Element Does

          1. Goal Selector: Select active goals, track progress percent, and see days remaining.
          2. 4-Bar Friction Profile: Balances cognitive and physical friction across Time, Money, Effort, and Risk.
          3. Best Path Checklist: The lowest-resistance sequence of actions to reach your destination.
          4. Dream & Search: AI brainstorms intermediate milestones, and A* computes the optimal route.
          5. Interactive Canvas: Drag to organize nodes, click to inspect, or pan and zoom the live graph.
          6. State Inspector: Review checklists, take notes, and dream outward actions from this milestone.
          7. Sui & Walrus Proof: Syncs memory blobs to Walrus and stamps milestone verification on Sui.
